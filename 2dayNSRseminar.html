Reptilian Complex (Lizard Brain): The most powerful brain—responsible for survival and automatic responses.

Mammalian Brain (Monkey Brain): Emotional center.

Neocortex: Logical, rational thinking.

Real change happens in the unconscious mind—where 95% of your behavior is generated. NSR works directly with the unconscious to create permanent transformation.

The Womb of Eternity

A timeline therapy process used to re-imprint the voice of authority in your mind.

Fast and effective way to loosen the less-than-positive voices in your mind — including your self-talk. Negative words affect your feelings of self-worth and self-image.

Space Odyssey

A fast and effective spatial relocation strategy designed to reduce or eliminate negative emotional responses to memories.

Benefits

Failure, rejection, disappointment, and tragedy are easy to recall and relive. As you nullify the emotions tied to these memories, your life will be better in every way.

What Happens

You'll learn to "dissolve" the emotional charge of painful memories by viewing them from increasingly distant vantage points — from the clouds, to Pluto, to the edge of the universe — until the emotion reaches zero.

Results

  • You can no longer access the negative emotion in the same way
  • The memory no longer impacts your life adversely

Ultimate Result

Common Questions Addressed

FAQ
"What if it doesn't work?"

Meet the 3 prerequisites (play pretend, full absorption, have fun). Trust the instructor's psychology. And if something still doesn't land — try something else. Never give up. There is always a way.

"I can't visualize—can I still do this?"

Yes! We use "imagine" not "visualize." Approximately 5% of people can't visualize, but everyone can imagine. The process is designed to be inclusive of all learning styles.

"I'm too logical/smart for this."

Smart Person Syndrome is the #1 blocker. Allow your critical faculty to step aside. The more you analyze and critique, the more you block your own progress. Full absorption is the key.

"How long until I see results?"

Change happens instantly once the setup is complete — the unconscious mind can change that fast. The setup may take various amounts of time, but when change happens, it happens in an instant.

"What if I don't reach zero on an emotion?"

There are multiple tools to amplify the process — darker tint on the glasses, more speakers, bigger clouds. We don't stop until it's zero. My goal is always zero.

"Do I need prior experience?"

No. Just bring an open mind and willingness to participate. Everything is guided step by step. The only requirement is that you show up and play full out.
